# Characters

Updated 2026-09-28. Tags as in `docs/REFERENCES.md`: **[V]** verified with a cited source,
**[U]** unverified or a design choice.

**The test for a character: each one is a clock and a place.** The lamplighter *is* dusk and dawn
on a fixed route. The executioner lives at the city's edge. A good character tells you when you are
or where the boundary is, and moves through the city on a rhythm the player can learn.

## The owner's characters

### The lamplighter (light manager)

Lights and puts out the city's lamps by hand. A lamplighter figure is already in the game (see
`docs/FIDELITY-PLAN.md`, stream D2). He pairs with the knygnešys in the November-dusk version:
every lamp he lights is a small danger to the courier. When and whether lamps were hand-lit in 1902
is [U] (roadmap open question 3).

### The budelis (executioner)

The owner's idea: he lives in the **Subačius Gate**. But that gate was **demolished on 27 May
1801** [V] ¹, and no source found says an executioner lived there [U]. Three ways to keep him:

- **As a legend** in the 1900 city: old residents point to where the gate stood. Pairs with the
  ghost gates (`docs/gates.md`).
- **In the pre-1799 era**, where the gate still stands.
- **As real history nearer the date:** after the 1863 uprising, Governor-General Muravyov had its
  leaders hanged in public on **Lukiškės Square**, just west of the Old Town, and was nicknamed "the
  Hangman" [U, needs a source].

### The knygnešys (book courier)

Brief: [`knygnesys.md`](knygnesys.md). He carries banned Lithuanian publications from the Gate of
Dawn to the attic of St Nicholas' Church, 1902–03. Grounded in the "Twelve Apostles" circle, which
hid books in that attic from 1900 [V] ².

## Candidates (ideas, not researched)

| Character | Clock and place | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Bund courier** | Night; from a hidden press | The Bund was founded in secret in Vilna on 7 Oct 1897, and a clandestine print shop opened there that year [V] ³. He carries banned *ideas* (socialist Yiddish), the knygnešys banned *letters* (the Latin alphabet). Two couriers, one gorodovoy |
| **Šamesas** (synagogue beadle) | Before dawn; the Jewish quarter | Knocked on shutters to wake people for prayers. The mirror of the lamplighter: one puts the city to bed, one wakes it [U] |
| **Gorodovoy** (policeman) | All day; posts and patrols | Imperial authority, and the knygnešys's threat. Where posts stood c. 1902 is [U] |
| **Kaminkrėtys** (chimney sweep) | Morning; the rooftops | A legitimate reason to be up on the roofs. Touching one is good luck [U] |
| **Vandenvežys** (water carrier) | All day; through the courtyards | An iconic Vilne figure, on routes through yards few people see [U] |
| **Fotografas** (plate photographer) | Midday; the square | His glass plates are the kind of evidence this reconstruction is built from [U] |
| **Bokštininkas** (fire watch) | Always; a tower | The one legitimate view over the whole city; Vilnius burned repeatedly [U] |
| **Duonkepys** (baker) | 4 a.m.; the one lit window | Atmosphere with almost no mechanics [U] |
| **Beggar at the Gate of Dawn** | All day; the only surviving gate | Sees everyone who enters the city that way; the information broker [U] |

**A proposed spine:** the lamplighter and the šamesas bracket the day, the knygnešys gives the
player something to do inside it, the gorodovoy something to avoid, and the budelis stands at the
edge. Five roles, four of which the town would need as ambience anyway.
